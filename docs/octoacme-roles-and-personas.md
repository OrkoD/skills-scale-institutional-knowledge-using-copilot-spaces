# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate sprint and milestone execution across teams, ensuring dependencies are tracked and delivery commitments are met.

### Responsibilities
- Own sprint and milestone execution across teams
- Track cross-team dependencies, blockers, and risks
- Coordinate escalation paths and mitigation actions
- Maintain delivery status artifacts and dashboarding
- Facilitate coordination with engineering managers and product leads

### Goals
- Improve predictability of delivery
- Reduce cross-team blockers and unplanned work
- Keep milestone commitments visible and actionable

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Works closely with Project Managers and Product Managers to align priorities and status
- Coordinates release timing with the Release Coordinator
- Escalates unresolved engineering blockers to engineering managers

---

## Product Operations Lead

### Role Summary
Product Operations drives process consistency, operating rhythms, and tooling around planning, release, and performance measurement.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and evolve operating processes, templates, and playbooks
- Track planning artifacts, cadence, and process health metrics
- Support teams in adopting documented processes and improvement practices
- Coordinate process rollouts and team enablement

### Goals
- Increase process adoption and reduce ad-hoc variation
- Improve visibility into planning and release rhythms

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Partners with Product Managers and the Documentation Steward to keep process artifacts current
- Supports the Delivery Manager with cadence and operational metrics
- Helps Project Managers maintain consistent execution patterns

---

## Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Coordinators manage release readiness, sequencing, and communication across engineering, QA, and operations.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release schedules and deployment plans
- Coordinate release sign-offs, checks, and rollback procedures
- Prepare release notes and stakeholder updates
- Run release readiness checks with QA and operations teams

### Goals
- Ensure smooth, well-communicated releases with minimal disruption
- Reduce release-risk and post-deployment issues caused by coordination gaps

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Works with Delivery Managers for sequencing and milestone readiness
- Aligns with Product Managers for customer-facing communication
- Coordinates with QA and operations for final readiness checks

---

## Documentation Steward

### Role Summary
The Documentation Steward owns the lifecycle of process documentation and templates so that the team can find accurate, current guidance when needed.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain process documents and templates used by the team
- Review and incorporate updates after retrospectives or process changes
- Support onboarding and change communication for process updates
- Coordinate with process owners to resolve conflicting guidance

### Goals
- Keep process documents accurate, discoverable, and easy to follow
- Reduce confusion caused by stale or inconsistent documentation

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Works with Project Managers and Product Operations to reflect current practices
- Collaborates with Product Managers to maintain alignment with project goals
- Uses the feedback from Delivery Managers and stakeholders to improve process clarity

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons represent external or cross-functional stakeholders and ensure their needs, constraints, and approvals are reflected in planning and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate stakeholder needs, constraints, and approval requirements
- Facilitate stakeholder involvement in planning and readiness checkpoints
- Help align external requirements with delivery realities
- Escalate unresolved stakeholder concerns to project leadership

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder alignment and reduce avoidable friction
- Ensure requirements are visible before decisions are made

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Project Managers to surface stakeholder needs
- Works with Delivery Managers when timing or dependencies require alignment
- Escalates unresolved concerns to leadership when needed

---

## Quality & Process Reviewer

### Role Summary
Quality & Process Reviewers validate that projects follow defined standards, quality gates, and agreed process expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Review process compliance and checklist completeness
- Identify process gaps and recommend improvements
- Participate in retrospectives with an eye toward continuous improvement
- Support governance and consistency across project work

### Goals
- Improve process quality and accountability
- Reduce avoidable rework caused by missing quality gates or unclear ownership

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Works with Project Managers, Delivery Managers, and the Documentation Steward to close process gaps
- Reviews release and execution health with operational stakeholders
- Helps ensure that process improvements are applied consistently

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles help clarify ownership, dependency management, stakeholder communication, and process accountability across project work.
